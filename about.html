About Me

My name is Ishan Kapoor, a Toronto-based freelance photographer with an avid passion for photography and creating/capturing images that tell stories, feel alive, are cinematic, and freeze a moment in time, giving that moment life beyond the moment. I love being able to capture emotions, energy, and atmospheres and create images that are aesthetically pleasing, meaningful, and evoke a variety of emotions.

I'm always looking to improve, experiment creatively, and push myself further with every shoot and every opportunity to pick up a camera. I really enjoy the variety of photography and its endless creative ventures, which is why I love working across a variety of styles and am constantly exploring new ideas and concepts.
